Understanding your experience

Start with context, not assumptions.

Sleep and stress influence nearly every part of wellbeing. Together we identify the habits, pressures, environment, and physical factors that may be keeping your nervous system on high alert.

This service may support conversations about:

  • Difficulty winding down or staying asleep
  • Stress-related fatigue and tension
  • Inconsistent energy through the day
  • Building realistic recovery habits

What is included

  1. Sleep and stress pattern assessment
  2. Daily rhythm and environment review
  3. Simple nervous-system support practices
  4. Nutrition and herbal options where appropriate
  5. Progress check-ins and plan refinement
