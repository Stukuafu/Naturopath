Our philosophy

Health support should feel personal, practical, and grounded.

In Harmony is a calm space to explore the patterns influencing your wellbeing and create a plan that makes sense in your real life.

Meet Rochelle

Curious about the why, focused on the useful.

Rochelle Sutton is presented here as a naturopath and medical herbalist who combines thoughtful listening with evidence-informed natural health care. Her role is not to promise a quick fix, but to help clients understand their options and build practical foundations for wellbeing.

Consultations are collaborative. Recommendations may include nutrition, sleep and stress support, medical herbalism, and discussion of appropriate testing or medical follow-up.

What guides the work

Four principles of care

01

Listen before recommending

Your experience, priorities, culture, resources, and existing healthcare shape the plan.

02

Use evidence with context

We consider quality of evidence, likely benefit, cost, safety, and whether an option is genuinely useful.

03

Make change sustainable

Small, well-chosen actions often matter more than complex routines that cannot be maintained.

04

Know when to collaborate

Naturopathic care works best alongside appropriate medical, dietetic, psychological, and allied health support.
