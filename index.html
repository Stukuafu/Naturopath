Your care journey

A clear, unhurried process

We start with the full context, agree on priorities together, and make each recommendation understandable and achievable.

Start with a free call
01

Start with your story

A 90-minute initial consultation explores your health history, current concerns, routines, goals, and any existing results.

02

Make sense of the patterns

We identify the most useful areas to focus on and explain the reasoning without overwhelming you.

03

Create your plan

You receive practical nutrition, lifestyle, and herbal recommendations matched to your pace and preferences.

04

Review and refine

Follow-up care tracks what is changing, removes what is not helping, and adjusts the plan as your needs evolve.
